# Semantic re-score — re-run of 2026-09-21, compared to RESCORE.md and the paper

`rescore.py` was released without `embed_lib.py`, `copy_contexts.json`, `rescore_results.json` or `embed_cache.json`, so it could not be run as released (R5-numbers.md §4.3). `embed_lib.py` has been reimplemented from `rescore.py`'s calling conventions and RESCORE.md's method description, and `copy_contexts.json` regenerated by `make_copy_contexts.py` with the frozen v1 scaffold engine under a hard gate (every question's recomputed exposed-gold count equals the stored `n_gold_exposed`; mean lexical ceiling 0.964477). `rescore.py` itself was then run **unmodified**.

Two independent checks say the reconstruction is faithful rather than merely plausible: the gold-title count is **778**, exactly the figure RESCORE.md records, and the total unique candidate-string count is within a few percent of RESCORE.md's 274,616 — both are emergent properties of `extract_candidates`, which was reconstructed from prose.
