## 1. What left the paper

**The two-edge composition study, entirely.** §`sec:composition`, §`sec:gemma` and
`fig:composition` are removed, together with every reference to them: the intro's
four-methods list, the MuSiQue single-premise lineage in §`sec:related` and
§`sec:ceiling-prior`, the limitations bullet and the conclusion's appeal to it.

The reason is evidential, not editorial. `uplift-results/recovered/RECOVERY.md` §v
establishes that no per-item rows, no 6,514-chain corpus and no mining script exist on any
ref, in the reflog, or in any scratchpad session; only the aggregate per-model deltas
already printed in the v8 prose and figure survive. Regenerating them is a new twelve-model
experiment, not a recovery, so the study is cut rather than described as pending.

The claim it carried — that exposure accounting and reasoning evaluation are separate
instruments — is now made two other ways. By argument: the A-requires-B-has-part-C example
in §`sec:ceiling` shows by construction that a question can require following two relations
while its answer name is already a string in the context, so exposure is 1, a perfect answer
scores 1, and gain over copy is exactly zero. And empirically: the semantic audit finds 4 of
240 credited items naming the gold target without asserting the asked-for relation, and 1
placing it in the wrong relation.

**The v5-to-v8 "old (complete-case)" control column** (true−raw +0.78 etc.) is deleted. It
matches neither the archived 2026-08-17 scores nor a fresh re-judge of the same completions,
and no artefact in the repository produces it.

**The five-arm control rerun and its fluent-noise arm**, which v5 to v8 reported as an ITT
column. Its completions, judge outputs and generating script were never written to disk
(`RECOVERY.md` §i, confirmed independently across all refs and the reflog). A rerun is in
progress but did not complete in time for this revision; it is named once, in the
conclusion's future-work paragraph, as the next control rather than as a pending result.
The `fig-controls` figure remains out of the paper.

## 3. What is new

**§`sec:audit`, a stratified semantic audit of the lexical matcher**, placed in Results after
in-domain delivery. Frame: all 11,360 (question, model, gold item) units of the released
sweep, regenerated to match `decomposition.json` exactly as a gate. Sample: 423 units,
stratified over matcher category then (template × gold type) then model, seed 42, with a
census of all 3 unexposed-but-credited units. Adjudicator: `openai/gpt-4.1` at t=0, a
six-label rubric with a fixed precedence order, every non-`absent` verdict required to cite a
verbatim span. **The adjudicator is a model, not a human**, stated in the section, the
limitations and the abstract; it is cross-family to nine of the ten swept models and the same
family as the tenth, whose self-preference risk is flagged with the per-model breakdown
released.

Findings: precision of the matcher's credit 0.971 [0.941, 0.986]; 0 of 60 false credit on
unexposed items; corrected exposed-item relational-correctness 0.905 [0.876, 0.922] against
the matcher's 0.931, a 2.5-point overstatement. Three mechanisms, the first two countable
without any judge: 208 credited units where the gold title is a substring of the question's
own subject name; seven gold titles whose words run together (`DeFi`, `EdDSA`, `GraphQL`,
`MultiAccessEdgeComputing`, `OpenXR`, `SustainabilityReporting`, `WebRTC`), unmatchable by
any spaced answer and therefore a systematic false omission identical for every model at
once; and T-TAX attachment errors, where credited-item precision is 0.938 against T-REL's
0.978. The deterministic any-collapse rule's 52 of 735 compliant omissions is reported as the
upper end of that correction, the judge being stricter at 0.017 quote-verified.

This section replaces every "a human audit is owed" passage in v8. The human pass itself is
prepared and not performed; its blind 120-unit sample and annotation guide ship unannotated.

## 4. Consistency pass

Each of these replaced a statement rather than qualifying it.

- `g > 0` is described as recovery beyond the copy baseline. The sign does not identify its
  source, and parametric memory, guessing and matcher asymmetry all produce it.
- Zero gain is nowhere equated with faithful delivery, including in `fig:gain`'s and
  `fig:ceiling`'s captions, both of which now say a zero net is equally consistent with no
  omissions and no recoveries, and with omissions and recoveries that cancel.
- The blanket "none of the four instruments is judge-free and deterministic" claim is gone.
- The intro's "the scalar can move under choices the counts are immune to" argument is gone;
  the companion pointer now says only that the note measures a choice task against a ranker
  baseline, a different instrument.
- "Generation must be evaluated as gain above the ceiling rather than against a bare model"
  becomes: report the exposure reference alongside quality and system baselines. Positive
  copy gain is not a prerequisite for useful generation.
- §`sec:accounting`'s "every quantity this paper reports is a function of them" becomes: the
  four counts determine the micro-averaged exposure and recovery metrics listed; macro
  scores, question-level uncertainty and judged outcomes need the per-question records.
- "Upper bound on the error rate" becomes: the raw omission count includes some compliant
  omissions, and the adjusted figure is a target-name omission rate, not a bound on semantic
  error. §`sec:audit` gives the semantic rate.
- "First-order insensitive to shared matcher error" becomes a stated possibility of partial
  cancellation, with no claim to an order of robustness, in both A2 and `fig:ceiling`.
- The wrapper confound is scoped: all control arms share the bare-block wrapper, so contrasts
  among them compare content within one instruction envelope; only loom−true is confounded by
  the live path's authority preamble.
- The promotion gate reads **name-complete under the matcher**, not answer-complete, at first
  use, with the audit cited for why the two come apart.
- The abstract loses the signed-rank parenthetical and every disclosure clause, and states
  results only. 1,794 characters; `ABSTRACT-arxiv-form.txt` beside this file.
- Limitations consolidated to one bullet per limitation, with no bullet restating a result
  and no owed-experiment bullets. A single four-item future-work paragraph closes the
  conclusion.
- `sec:suppression` moved into Results, after the ten-model sweep.
- Em-dashes removed from prose throughout.

`pdftotext` over the built PDF has zero hits for todo, originally, rerun, revised,
downgrade, historical, "no longer" and "our earlier reading". Three surviving hits are
justified: "corrected relational-correctness rate" is a statistical correction of an
estimate; "withdrawn" appears three times as the arXiv status of two cited third-party
