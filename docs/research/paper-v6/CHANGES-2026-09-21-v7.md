## New material

### §Routing — "A Second Domain: Typed Skill Routing"

An agent runtime routes each turn to one of 115 skills whose rubrics are shown to the judge
as options, plus `none`. Gold is one exposed rubric key, so exposure is total — stronger
than Study 1, where 3.6 % of gold lay outside the scaffold. The copy procedure is adapted to
a choice task as the best judge-free surface match over the exposed options (BM25; 384-d
cosine), each granted a decline rule whose threshold is oracle-tuned on the evaluated corpus.

**The section's first finding is a fault in its own instrument.** The first implementation
reported a lexical ceiling of 76.7 % and a judge advantage of +11.6 points. Two defects,
both inflating the judge:

1. **The threshold sweep could not find its own optimum.** A "decline below t" rule is a
   step function whose only breakpoints are the observed scores; the sweep evaluated a fixed
   41-point grid over the observed range and stepped over the maximising window
   (9.5715 < t <= 9.7420, narrower than one grid step). Ceiling -> 79.1 %, gain -> +9.3.
2. **Exclusion clauses were indexed as positive evidence.** 50 of 115 rubrics carry a clause
   saying what the option is *not* for. In one undifferentiated bag those clauses are dense
   in the vocabulary they disclaim, so a turn scored highest on the rubric that exists to
   reject it. Dropping each clause from its marker onward — strictly less use of the
   exposure — gives ceiling -> 83.7 %, gain -> +4.7.

Together they manufactured 8.1 of the original 11.6 points. Both are fixed in the released
rig (`crates/system-one/system-one-eval/src/copy.rs`) with regression tests that construct
each failure.

**Result: three judges, one repaired ceiling, one corpus** (Table `tab:routing`).

| judge | top-1 | vs lexical 83.7 % | exact p | vs embedding 69.8 % | exact p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 421M encoder, shared head budget | 53.5 | -30.2 | — | -16.3 | — |
| 4B cross-encoder, independent scoring | 88.4 | +4.7 | 0.455 | +18.6 | 0.00086 |
| Cloud judge (`jev-1.13.0`) | 93.0 | +9.3 | 0.057 | +23.3 | < 0.001 |

The two competent judges are not distinguishable from each other (8 discordant against 4,
exact p = 0.39). Exact power at n = 86 against the observed lexical effect is 0.50; about
157 turns would be needed for 80 %. Statistics are exact two-sided McNemar, which is what
the paper's pre-specified conditional signed-rank test reduces to on a ±1 paired difference.
The earlier draft used a normal-approximation Wald interval that appears nowhere in §Method.

**Decomposition:** against the repaired lexical ceiling the 4B judge's advantage is exactly
zero on near-neighbour picking (n=50) and exactly zero on early-discriminator turns (n=54),
with all of it on boundary (+16.7, n=12) and late-discriminator (+12.5, n=32) turns. No
subgroup survives Holm within its partition, and the two smallest carry four discordant
pairs each, where the two-sided exact p floors at 0.125.

**Label audit:** only 26 of 86 turns can move a paired comparison (22 discordant + 4
both-wrong); a relabelling inside the 60 both-correct turns is exactly neutral. The complete
movable set was re-read against the rubrics' own text. One corpus error was found and
corrected (turn 14, `browser` -> `browser-automation`); turn 28 is its mirror and was kept
against the judge; turn 18 is contestable in the judge's favour and was left standing. The
correction moves the lexical gain by 2.3 points and changes no conclusion.

### §Ideal State — "A Private Corporate Data Lake"

Seven properties (D1–D7), each marked *measured* or *specified*, around one through-line:
the same judge-free control at every seam, so each decision about private data is measured
against a verbatim copy of its own input before it is trusted.

### Cut from the draft during preparation

Confidence-calibration, escalation-cascade and threshold-sweep tables; the production
reweighting; the provenance (tier-A) split. Each was either about the engine rather than the
instrument, structurally incapable of significance at its n, or retracted on re-analysis —

sentence rather than a table: nine passes over the same 86 turns are one corpus re-scored
nine times, the full 5.8-point span is five turns, and the widest pairwise contrast is
itself non-significant.

## Reproduction

`system-one-eval run --concurrency 1 --timeout-ms 120000` (the local 4B engine is serialised
by a tokeniser lock; the default concurrency queues past the timeout), then
`system-one-eval copy-ceiling --from-report … --ceilings both`. Embeddings are requested one
text per call: the estate's endpoint returns global batch offsets in `data[].index` when
concurrent requests merge. A remote backend now requires a bearer token and the rig refuses
to run without one — an unauthenticated remote run answers 403 on every case and is
otherwise indistinguishable in the report from the backend being unavailable, which cost
this preparation several hours.

## Caveats

- The routing corpus is self-authored and single-seed. It is the binding limitation and is
  stated in the section, in the limitations and here.
- The cloud judge is non-deterministic across identical requests (confidence 0.95/0.92/0.93
  on repeats). Its column is a single pass and should carry repeats before any comparison is
  drawn between the two judges.
- Nothing here has been peer reviewed; it is an arXiv replacement.
