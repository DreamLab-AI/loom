\section{Ideal State: A Private Corporate Data Lake}\label{sec:datalake}
One estate ran every study in this paper, yet the findings constrain a whole class of system: an organisation holding a large private corpus that must be answered from, routed over and kept current, without the content leaving the organisation. This section states what that system should look like if the measurements above are taken seriously, as a set of properties, since an architecture would over-specify what the evidence supports, and marks which of them the present work has measured and which it has only specified.

\paragraph{Through-line, and why it is the highest-value one.} Every property below is a consequence of a single move: \textbf{put the same judge-free control at every seam of the lake, so that each decision the system makes about private data is measured against what a verbatim copy of its own input would already achieve, before anyone is asked to trust it.} Promotion of a fact into the corpus, the choice of serving path, the routing of a request, the keeping or dropping of a transcript: each is a place where a model's output could be mistaken for judgement, and each admits the same per-item, deterministic ceiling and the same signed gain. The justification is empirical rather than aesthetic. Across ten models the gain over copy on the serving path was negative throughout (\S\ref{sec:tenmodel}), so a lake that pays for synthesis over a curated corpus is paying for reasoning the instrument shows is not occurring; the promotion gate refused a plausible enrichment that a judge would have waved through (\S\ref{sec:casestudy}); and on the routing seam the same instrument said what a judge adds, located it in declining and late-clause discrimination, and flagged corpus errors it had no way to know about (\S\ref{sec:routing}). One control, applied everywhere, converts a trust problem into a measurement problem, and the control costs nothing that scales with the model: a copy, a lexical rank, a subtraction. That is why it outranks the other six properties. Curation, egress control and reversible writes are ordinary good practice that any organisation can adopt without this paper; the ceiling is what makes them checkable, and it is the one property that tells the organisation where its money is and is not doing work.

\paragraph{Properties of the ideal state.}
\begin{enumerate}[topsep=2pt,itemsep=3pt,leftmargin=2em,label=\textbf{D\arabic*}]
\item \textbf{A curated core with a promotion gate.} (The through-line applied to admitting facts.) The corpus is the curated, provenance-carrying subset of the lake that the organisation is prepared to serve as fact; the rest of the lake is raw material. Candidates enter through a gate that computes the copy ceiling before promotion, so an extraction whose recall does not exceed what its own source already exposes is rejected as restatement (\S\ref{sec:casestudy}). \emph{Measured:} the podcast assertion pipeline and its judged-page gate.
\item \textbf{A delivery-first serving path.} (The through-line applied to answering.) The default answer path is retrieval plus a static scaffold, and its quality is reported as gain over copy by population rather than as raw recall. Synthesis machinery is admitted only where that gain is positive and located, since the alternative is paying for reasoning that the ceiling shows is not happening (\S\ref{sec:tenmodel}, \S\ref{sec:controls}). \emph{Measured:} in-domain delivery fidelity; out-of-domain non-regression. \emph{Open:} the web-search baseline and attribution precision (\S\ref{sec:bar}).
\item \textbf{Typed decisions at every seam, on local judges.} (The through-line applied to deciding.) Routing, keep-or-drop, verification and re-ranking are small typed judgements, answered by a judge that scores options independently so that declining is a threshold rather than a competitor, and each judgement is shipped with a fair copy ceiling and read by population (\S\ref{sec:routing}). A judge-free ranker answers first and the judge is called on escalation; on the routing corpus that cascade matches the judge at two-fifths of its latency. The implementation is the typed-decision façade of \S\ref{sec:routing}: consumers speak one wire protocol (a choice, an ordered score or a probability), and the judge behind it is whichever engine last won the eval. \emph{Measured:} routing, on one small corpus, through that façade. \emph{Specified:} keep-or-drop over transcripts, where the same instrument applies and the decline asymmetry is sharper; and the served node's own promotion and judged-page gates, which still run on prose-emitting LLM judges and are the next seam to move behind the façade.
\item \textbf{A hard egress boundary with a swappable model behind a façade.} No corpus content, transcript or judgement state leaves the boundary; the model is a URL behind a stable façade and is replaced on the strength of a measurement, with no change on the consumer side. The routing replication was itself such a swap: the failing engine and its successor sat behind one wire protocol, and the eval decided between them where a vendor's claim could not have. \emph{Measured:} the swap and its cost; \emph{specified:} the boundary as policy.
\item \textbf{Standing instrumentation, not one-off evaluation.} (The through-line as an operating discipline.) Every served path reports its ceiling, its signed gain, its population breakdown and its power, as a matter of course; the ten-model and routing tables are what that reporting looks like. Where a ranker and a judge disagree, the items are queued for label audit rather than counted silently against either (\S\ref{sec:routing}). \emph{Measured:} the instrument; \emph{specified:} its standing operation.
\item \textbf{A gated, reversible write path.} Additions to the corpus carry provenance, pass the promotion gate, and can be withdrawn; the lifecycle in \S\ref{sec:lifecycle} is the shape, and thin-page enrichment is the example of a plausible addition that the gate correctly refused. \emph{Measured:} the refusal; \emph{specified:} reversibility at scale.
\item \textbf{Stated limits.} The system delivers vetted knowledge faithfully and attributably at low cost. Discovery is outside its remit and should stay outside its marketing: on unexposed items the grounded path recovers less than the bare model does. A lake that promises reasoning over private data is promising the thing the ceiling shows is absent.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{What aggregate means here.} None of the seven properties is novel on its own; several are ordinary data-governance practice. The claim is about their composition: that a private data lake whose every seam is a typed judgement with a copy ceiling beside it, whose serving path is delivery-first by measurement, and whose corpus admits facts only past that same ceiling, is a system whose behaviour can be stated and checked, which is a stronger position than one that has to be trusted. The present work has measured the instrument, the serving path on one corpus, one promotion gate, one model swap and one routing seam. It has not run the seven properties together as one deployment, and that composition is the next thing to measure, with the same discipline: ceiling first, gain by population, power stated, and the model behind a door.
