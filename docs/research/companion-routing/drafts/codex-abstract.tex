\begin{abstract}
\noindent
When a language model answers from a curated corpus via graph-based retrieval, reported grounding uplift mixes faithful \emph{delivery} of facts already shown to the model with \emph{reasoning} over injected structure, because gold answers derive from the same corpus. We propose the \emph{copy ceiling}, the recall a verbatim copy of the shown context would achieve, and the signed \emph{gain over copy}: a deterministic, judge-free, per-item control reported beside recall and paired with a seed-disjoint placebo. On a production ontology-serving node, mean unaided recall is 0.26 (range 0.151--0.375) and grounded recall about 0.92, yet across ten models from eight developer families gain over copy is uniformly negative ($-0.067$ to $-0.022$). A decomposition of 11{,}360 gold items explains why: three unexposed items are recovered in total, context utilisation spans 0.900--0.955, and models differ only in how much exposed gold they drop. On items the scaffold does not expose, recovery falls from 0.121 bare to 0.004 grounded: injection displaces parametric recall the bare model demonstrably has. The uplift is delivery, measured rather than inferred. A paired study on the same node, varying only the serving path, lifts judged quality by $+0.27$ pooled (exact $p=0.002$) and $+0.79$ where curation is deepest. A uniform-budget control rerun with a length-matched fluent-noise arm shows the scaffold beats no context ($+0.75$, Holm-significant) but does not establish that the specific retrieved content beats generic on-corpus text. Out of domain the general-set interval ($+0.05$, $[0.00,+0.13]$) lies inside a pre-specified $\pm0.25$ margin (58/60 pairs tie). A two-edge composition test with single-premise controls separates models that compose exposed structure from those that do not; a second local family replicates both findings. Applied to a second domain, typed skill routing over 115 shown rubrics, the fair ceiling locates a 4B cross-encoder's gain in declining and in late-clause discrimination ($+25.0$ and $+18.8$ points) while ordinary near-neighbour picking among exposed options gains $+4.0$, and ranker--judge disagreement doubles as a label audit that corrected one gold label. Evaluations whose gold derives from the injected corpus should report a copy ceiling as standard.
