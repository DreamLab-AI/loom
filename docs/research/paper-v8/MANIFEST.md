# Table-to-artefact manifest — paper v8 (21 September 2026)

Every table and figure in `main.tex`, mapped to the artefact that holds its rows and the
script that produces it. Paths are relative to the repository root
(`https://github.com/DreamLab-AI/loom`). Written in response to the external review's
closing request: "Pin this version to a commit and publish a table-to-artifact manifest."

**Pinned at:** commits `ce137f5` (routing-seam evidence) and `0acd8da` (per-row evidence
for Studies 1 and 2 and the judged arms), both 21 September 2026. There is no git tag.

**Reading the status column**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **released** | Rows and generator are both under version control; a reader can re-derive the numbers. |
| **rows only** | Rows are released; the generating script is not (or has been deleted). Checkable, not re-runnable. |
| **script only** | The generator is released; its saved output is not. Re-runnable against a live service, not checkable offline. |
| **not released** | Neither rows nor output exist in the repository. The figures rest on our reporting. |

**Two standing caveats that apply to every row below.** `uplift-results/` is listed in
`.gitignore`; the 278 evidence files are force-added, so a fresh clone gets them but
`git status` will not notice new ones. And `app/data/` is **not** released, which matters
because `app/data/scaffold-index.json` (7.9 MB) is a load-bearing input to four scripts:
every claim of the form "the scaffold is regenerated byte-identically" depends on a file
a reader does not have.

- **Analysis script:** `bench/sweep/analyse.py`
  (`--outdir uplift-results/sweep --resamples 10000`).

  `bench/sweep/launch-sweep.sh`; model list in `bench/sweep/models.tsv`; logs in

### `tab:live` — Study 2, the production-node paired study (§Production-node paired study)

Per set and pooled: n, means, paired mean difference, bootstrap CI, exact signed-rank p,

- **Rows:** `uplift-results/paper-v2/live-results.jsonl` (234 rows) —
  **mutated in place** by `tools/paper/retry_empties.py` for the 1536→4096 re-runs, so

- **Judge scores:** `uplift-results/paper-v2/judged.json` (360 elements, `gpt-4.1` pass).
- **Aggregate:** `uplift-results/paper-v2/analysis.json`.
- **Scripts:** `tools/paper/live_harness.py` (generation) →
  `tools/paper/retry_empties.py` (budget re-runs) → `tools/paper/judge_v2.py`
  (judging) → `tools/paper/analyze.py --scores … --live … --out …` (statistics;
  implements `exact_signed_rank`).
- **Status: released.**
- **Note for anyone reading `analyze.py`:** `cliffs_delta` is a documented compatibility
  alias for `sign_dominance`; the paper's effect size is the rank-biserial from
  `decomposition.json`, not this field.

### `tab:controls` — Negative-control contrasts, old and new (§Negative controls)

Eleven contrasts across six arms, in two columns: *old* (complete-case, 1536-token
budget) and *new* (intention-to-treat, common retry policy, fluent-noise arm added).

- **Old column — rows:** `uplift-results/paper-v2/control-results.jsonl` (228 rows,
  four arms: true, shuffled, masked, irrelevant), judged in
  `uplift-results/paper-v2/judged.json`.
- **Old column — scripts:** `tools/paper/control_harness.py`
  (`--sets arcane,thin --out uplift-results/paper-v2`; `MAX_TOKENS=1536`; question sets
  hard-coded to `uplift-results/general/{arcane,thin,general}-questions.json`) and
  `tools/paper/analyze_controls.py`, which merges its output **in place** into
  `analysis.json` under the key `"controls"`.
- **Old column status: released.**
- **New (ITT) column: NOT RELEASED.** The five-arm common-retry-policy rerun (4096
  tokens with 8192-token retries), its `gemini-3.1-pro-preview` judge outputs, the
  fluent-noise arm and the script that generated them were never written to disk. The
  commit that published this evidence, `0acd8da`, says so in its own message. A repository
  search for `*rerun*`, `*fluent*`, `*noise*`, `*gemini-3.1*` and `*common-retry*` returns
  exactly one file, `uplift-results/rerun-scaffold.log`, which is unrelated.
- **Consequence:** every ITT figure in this table, and the per-arm *n* ≈ 50–55 quoted in
  the section, is **reported-but-not-released**. So is the common-intersection sensitivity
  analysis the section names as owed. Disclosed in the abstract, §Introduction,
  §Negative controls and §Limitations.

## What is not released, and where the paper says so

Six gaps. All six are now disclosed in the manuscript; this manifest and the §Introduction
release statement enumerate the same list.

| # | Absent | Disclosed in |
|---|---|---|
| i | **Control rerun (ITT) rows** — the 5-arm common-retry-policy cohort, its judge outputs and its generating script | abstract, §Introduction, §Negative controls, §Limitations |
| ii | **Routing per-item rows**, two of three engines | §Introduction, §Companion work, and `uplift-results/routing/README.md` |
| iii | **Semantic re-score** results and embedding cache | §Introduction, §Semantic re-score, §Limitations |
| iv | **Sweep generating harness** (`bench/bench_ontology_uplift.py`, deleted) | §Introduction |
| v | **Two-edge composition corpus**, mining script and six-arm rows — the whole of `fig:composition` and its section | §Introduction, §Two-Edge Composition, §Limitations |
| vi | **Paraphrase stress-set** and its per-arm ceilings | §Introduction, §Vocabulary mismatch, §Limitations |

Items **v** and **vi** sit behind results the conclusion presents as standing on their own,
which is why the §Limitations bullet singles them out. Items **i**, **v** and **vi** are the
three where nothing at all exists on disk; **ii**, **iii** and **iv** are partial, and each
could be closed by a re-run rather than by re-doing the study.

Releasing the rows for **v** and **vi** is the cheapest remaining improvement to the paper's
reproducibility: both were generated by scripts that still exist, and neither needs a model
call to re-derive its exposure figures.
