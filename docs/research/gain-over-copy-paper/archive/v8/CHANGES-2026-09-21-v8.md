# Paper v8 — changes from v7 (2026-09-21)

Scope: the response to the first external review (`REVIEW-2026-09-21-external.md`, of the
31 pp v7). The full item-by-item disposition is `REMEDIATION-2026-09-21.md`; this is the
short account of what moved.

**31 pp → 26 pp.** Almost nothing was added. The paper got smaller, and the claims inside it
got considerably narrower.

## What left the paper

- **§Ideal State: A Private Corporate Data Lake** (v7 §18, D1–D7). Cut entirely. The
  reviewer's judgement — "the weakest part of the manuscript" — was accepted. What
  survives is §Open Axes (two measured axes, two specified and unmeasured) and §Companion
  Work and Deferred Consequences, which defers the architectural argument and names the
  evidence it would need: a matched comparison against a direct graph query and flat-text
  retrieval at equal budgets, and an accounting of curation effort against query volume.
- **§A Second Domain: Typed Skill Routing.** Split out into a 5 pp companion note,
  `docs/research/companion-routing/`, with its evidence manifest at
  `uplift-results/routing/README.md` and the rig snapshot at `tools/routing-eval/`. The
  reviewer's finding that the routing section silently swapped a ranker baseline in for
  the exposure scalar is accepted in its strong form: the two quantities are now in two
  documents, under two names, declared different instruments in three places in the paper.
- **§Write-Path Lifecycle stays.** It was cut mid-pass and folded into the case study as one
  paragraph, then restored as its own section on an author decision recorded in the ledger.
  It carries the Event Sourcing / CQRS materialised-view framing scoped to the assertion
  layer, the per-assertion fingerprinting that makes promotion idempotent, and footnotes
  naming the VisionFlow ingest pipeline and the ontology-bridge governed propose/approve
  path. The reviewer's suggestion to move the system narrative out was declined on this
  point: the instrument travels, the write-path result does not, and it is only checkable
  against the stack it was measured on.
- **Three figures.** `fig-controls` (the historical control panel the reviewer said
  "competes with its replacement"), `fig-forest` and `fig-casestudy` (both redundant with
  the tables they sat beside). Their sources remain in `figures/` but are no longer
  `\input`. `fig-ecosystem` and `fig-lifecycle` were cut in the same pass and then
  restored with the lifecycle section. Surviving: `fig:ecosystem`, `fig:ceiling`,
  `fig:composition`, `fig:lifecycle`, plus the inline `fig:gain`.
- **The two exposure-decomposition tables** merged into one.

## What was reworded

- **The thesis is no longer a claim about reasoning.** §The Copy Ceiling now carries the
  reviewer's own counterexample (A requires B, B has part C) and concedes the narrow
  statement: *this recall score does not establish reasoning beyond answer-name exposure*.
  The conclusion adds that it equally does not establish its absence. "Rules out",
  "absence of reasoning" and "lower bound" appear nowhere in v8; "detector" survives once,
  as a negation.
- **§Analysis: Exposure, not Reasoning → §What the Accounting Attributes.** The old title
  asserted the conclusion the reviewer said the instrument cannot support.
- **§Grounding Suppresses Parametric Recall** is promoted to a section of its own and
  narrowed in the same move. The "roughly thirty times worse outside the corpus" claim is
  deleted; a dedicated paragraph states that "absent from the retrieved scaffold" is not
  "outside the corpus", and that obedience to an authoritative source may be the desired
  behaviour. The four-condition wrapper experiment that would explain the mechanism is
  written in as the single experiment we would run next.
- **"Composer" and "anti-composer" are gone.** The composition groups are relabelled
  "three exploratory, configuration-specific behavioural groups, not a capability ladder
  and not model properties", with the multiplicity non-adjustment stated and the headroom
  caveat generalised beyond GPT-5.6.
- **"Delivery" is scoped at first use** to recall of exposed gold target names and nothing
  more. "Vetted" and "attributably" are gone; "uniform budget" is gone, replaced
  throughout by "a common retry policy" with the cost consequence stated.
- **The any-of gold-set correction is published.** Recomputed from the released rows: 52
  of the 735 exposed-but-omitted items are compliant omissions, so "one exposed item in
  fourteen is dropped" becomes one in 15.5, and the item-level figure is labelled an upper
  bound on an error rate rather than an error rate.
- **The message-placement claim in v7 was simply wrong** and is corrected. Both the served
  node and the control harness inject into a *system* message. The real envelope
  difference is the wrapper text: the live path prepends an authority instruction, the
  controls do not, so no contrast isolates content from instruction strength.
- **11,360 now carries its structure everywhere it appears** — abstract, introduction,
  ceiling, re-score, results, limitations, conclusion — as 1,136 target instances under
  each of ten models, not 11,360 independent facts.
- **The macro ceiling (0.964) and the item-pooled exposed fraction (0.933)** are declared
  non-interchangeable in §Limitations, and the one surviving conflation in §In-domain was
  corrected in the closing pass.
- **§Relation to prior instruments** was rewritten to concede precedent component by
  component: RAGChecker for the decomposition, the reader–context diagnostic as "the
  closest comparator overall", Sufficient Context as asking a strictly stronger question,
  MuSiQue as the origin of the single-premise logic.

## Evidence

Two commits put the paper's per-row evidence under version control for the first time:

- **`ce137f5`** — the routing-seam evidence (`uplift-results/routing/`: 86-turn corpus,
  115 rubrics, mined rows, per-run reports, embedding caches, 21 analysis scripts), the
  companion note, and the rig snapshot `tools/routing-eval/`. 74 files.
- **`0acd8da`** — everything else under `uplift-results/`: the frozen 510-question set,
  the full ten-model sweep, the production-study rows and both judge passes, the historical
  control cohort, and the judged-arm study directories. 232 files. 278 files are now tracked.

Excluded, deliberately: `uplift-results/ingest/` (52 MB of corpus SQL and concept records,
not per-row evidence) and `app/data/`.

The table-to-artefact manifest the review asked for is `MANIFEST.md`. It itemises **six**
release gaps: the control rerun's ITT rows (never written to disk), two of the routing
note's three judge rows, the semantic re-score's saved results and cache, the deleted
sweep-generating harness, the two-edge composition corpus, and the paraphrase stress-set.
The last two were found while writing the manifest. **All six are now disclosed in the
manuscript** — in the §Introduction release statement, in each owning section (and, for composition, in the figure
caption), and as one explicit enumerated list in the §Limitations "Release completeness"
bullet. Composition and the stress-set are singled out
there because they sit behind results the conclusion presents as standing on their own.

The control-rerun disclosure appears in four places: the abstract, the §Introduction
release statement, §Negative controls, and a dedicated §Limitations bullet. The team's
decision was to keep those numbers with that disclosure rather than cut them.

There is **no git tag** on this version. The §Introduction release statement names the two
commits directly rather than promising one, and v7's claim that the semantic re-score's
evidence was released — which was false — is corrected.
