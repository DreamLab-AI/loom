## 3. What is new

**§`sec:audit`, a stratified semantic audit of the lexical matcher**, placed in Results after
in-domain delivery. Frame: all 11,360 (question, model, gold item) units of the released
sweep, regenerated to match `decomposition.json` exactly as a gate. Sample: 423 units,
stratified over matcher category then (template × gold type) then model, seed 42, with a
census of all 3 unexposed-but-credited units. Adjudicator: `openai/gpt-4.1` at t=0, a
six-label rubric with a fixed precedence order, every non-`absent` verdict required to cite a
verbatim span. **The adjudicator is a model, not a human**, stated in the section, the
limitations and the abstract; it is cross-family to nine of the ten swept models and the same
family as the tenth, whose self-preference risk is flagged with the per-model breakdown
released.

Findings: precision of the matcher's credit 0.971 [0.941, 0.986]; 0 of 60 false credit on
unexposed items; corrected exposed-item relational-correctness 0.905 [0.876, 0.922] against
the matcher's 0.931, a 2.5-point overstatement. Three mechanisms, the first two countable
without any judge: 208 credited units where the gold title is a substring of the question's
own subject name; seven gold titles whose words run together (`DeFi`, `EdDSA`, `GraphQL`,

any spaced answer and therefore a systematic false omission identical for every model at

0.978. The deterministic any-collapse rule's 52 of 735 compliant omissions is reported as the
upper end of that correction, the judge being stricter at 0.017 quote-verified.

This section replaces every "a human audit is owed" passage in v8. The human pass itself is
prepared and not performed; its blind 120-unit sample and annotation guide ship unannotated.

## 1. False credit, split into two questions

The "0 of 60" units are `n00`: unexposed **and uncredited by the matcher**, so they cannot
establish a false-credit rate at all. The abstract, `sec:audit` and the conclusion now put
two separate questions with two separate pieces of evidence.

- **Undetected recovery.** On the 60 unexposed-and-uncredited items the judge finds no
  hidden correct relational answer, 0 of 60, interval upper bound about 6 %.
- **False credit.** Answered only by the `n01` census. All three unexposed-but-credited
  items in the frame fail relational adjudication (two `absent`, one `name_only` under the
  v2 gate), so credit beyond exposure is rare — three units in 11,360 — and on this census
  its precision is zero.

Frequency and precision are stated separately throughout. "Essentially never spurious on
unexposed items" is deleted from `What this licenses`.

## 2. The quote gate is one symmetric policy

`sec:audit` now describes the protocol rather than its outcome: every failed citation, in
every stratum, is re-asked once for a verbatim supporting span with the rubric and item
unchanged; a verdict that still cannot supply one is `unresolved`, neither kept nor demoted.
The policy never consults the matcher's own category.

Of 281 verdicts required to cite, 252 verify first pass and the 29 failures split 11
repaired / 18 unresolved. The v1 split is reproduced from evidence rather than assumption:
all 11 repairs fall in the credited stratum (6 verbatim, 5 under the matcher's own
normalisation) and all 18 concessions in the uncredited strata (14 `n10`, 4 `n00`).

The acceptance rule is stated with the protocol: a re-asked span verifies if it matches the
answer character for character, or under the matcher's own whitespace and punctuation
normalisation, so v2 can never demote a unit v1 would have kept. Its sensitivity is stated
too. Under a strictly verbatim rule the 5 normalisation-only repairs would be unresolved
instead, all 5 in the credited stratum and all carrying a `correct_relation` verdict, and
credit precision would read 0.950 (228/240) against the matcher, 0.971 (233/240) for it and
0.970 (228/235) with them excluded. Both verification flags,
`repaired_verifies_verbatim_whitespace_only` and `repaired_verifies_matcher_normalised`, are
recorded per unit in `quote-gate-v2.jsonl`.

Every audit rate is now read three ways — unresolved verdict stands, withdrawn, excluded.
Credited-item rates are invariant (precision 0.971 in all three). The `Omissions` paragraph
is restated in that form: true omissions 0.708 / 0.825 / 0.802, named-at-all 0.225 / 0.108 /
0.123, compliant alternative 0.042 / 0.017 / 0.019.

Source: `uplift-results/semantic-audit/SUMMARY.md` §3A, §3B, `quote-gate-v2.jsonl`.

## 3. The correction is an estimate, and its interval is specified

0.931 to 0.905 is described as an estimated correction under this adjudicator and this
protocol, never as a known overstatement. Three qualifications sit beside the number: the
adjudicator is a model and the demotion rule is a quote policy; both strata are sampled and
their observations repeat questions and gold targets across ten models; and the denominator
is lexically exposed items while the judged quantity is a property of answers, not a measure
of context sufficiency.

The estimator and interval are stated in full: R = (9,865·p11 + 735·p10)/10,600 with
p11 = 233/240 and p10 = 3/120, interval from a stratified cluster bootstrap resampling
questions within stratum (184 `n11` clusters, 59 `n10`, 10,000 draws, seed 42), giving
[0.884, 0.923]. Model-clustering gives [0.886, 0.924]. The naive propagated Wilson
[0.876, 0.922] is reported for comparison only and named invalid for this design. R moves
0.9052 to 0.9054 across the three readings.

Source: `SUMMARY.md` §4A.

## 1. How the control came to be built (§`sec:intro`)

A four-sentence paragraph near the end of the introduction, before the methods list: a deployed

## 2. What the accounting does not price: controlled delivery (§`sec:analysis`)

One paragraph, placed before "Consequence 1", on the intended practical value of controlled
knowledge delivery: an organisation wants its maintained knowledge answerable through a replaceable
model, with control over the source, its versions and the route by which an addition becomes
authoritative; selecting, maintaining, governing and delivering that material are substantial system
responsibilities; exposure accounting identifies which part of an observed result they explain and
does not measure what they are worth to operate; and a negative gain over copy is not an argument
against them. The paragraph closes on the reviewer's three-way separation, three statements that can
be made confidently (versioned curated material through a replaceable interface; the scaffold
